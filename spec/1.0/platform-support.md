# PunPun 1.x platform support tiers

The machine-readable policy is `support/platforms.json`.

- **Tier 1:** Linux x86-64, release-blocking.
- **Tier 2:** Arch x86-64 and Windows x86-64, official targets/packages but individually qualified only after their real release-tag workflows pass.
- **Tier 3:** macOS and ARM64, unsupported in 1.0.
